Web Developer (VHSE)

Overview: This cutting-edge vocational course focuses on the fundamentals and advanced concepts of web design and development. Students learn to build, design, and manage modern websites.

Skills Acquired: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, and basic back-end programming. This prepares students for immediate entry into the IT industry or higher studies in Computer Science.

Dietetic Aid (VHSE)

Overview: A highly specialized healthcare course aimed at understanding human nutrition, dietary planning, and health management.

Skills Acquired: Students learn meal planning, therapeutic diets, clinical nutrition, and food safety, opening pathways to careers in hospitals and wellness centers.

LTPM (VHSE)

Overview: Livestock & Poultry Management provides comprehensive knowledge in the agricultural and veterinary sectors.

Skills Acquired: Animal husbandry, farm management, disease control, and agribusiness operations, promoting self-employment.
