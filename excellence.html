🏆 Our Excellence 🏆

Celebrating the brilliant achievements of our students and faculty.

🎓 Academic Brilliance

GVHSS Moolamattom has a proud legacy of outstanding academic results. Year after year, our students consistently secure top grades (Full A+) in the SSLC and VHSE board examinations. Our dedicated faculty ensures that no student is left behind, resulting in a phenomenal pass percentage that ranks us among the top schools in the district.

🎨 Arts & Cultural Fest (Kalolsavam)

We believe in nurturing creative talents. Our school is a dominant force at the Sub-District and District School Kalolsavam. From traditional dance forms like Mohiniyattam and Margamkali to literary competitions, our students have brought home the overall championship trophy multiple times, showcasing the rich cultural spirit of our campus.

🏅 Sports & Athletics

Physical fitness and teamwork are core values here. Our athletes regularly compete at the State Athletics Meet. With excellent training and dedication, we have produced district-level champions in track events, football, and volleyball, instilling a strong sense of sportsmanship in our youth.

💡 Innovation & Technology (YIP & Little Kites)

We are shaping the innovators of tomorrow. Our students have received state-level recognition and funding through the Young Innovators Programme (YIP) for their groundbreaking project prototypes. Furthermore, our Little Kites unit is recognized as one of the most active in the state, excelling in animation, hardware programming, and IT skills.
